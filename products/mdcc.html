Mobile Distributed Command Centre

The fusion layer, built as a platform you can drive to the edge of an operation. It carries the whole intelligence stack — compute, storage, networking and Cortex — with every stage of processing running locally, so the platform depends on nothing outside itself.

03 · Specifics

What the platform carries

Compute, storage, networking and the Cortex software, with every stage of processing running locally. One operator supervises six to twenty ISR aircraft while the vision layer watches every feed, remembers what each patch of ground looked like on the last pass, and escalates only what has actually changed.

The significant engineering claim is the zero external dependency line in the specification. It means the platform holds its own models, its own historical observation store and its own inference capacity. Nothing in the assessment path requires a link to a data centre. When the bearers degrade, what is lost is dissemination range — not the picture itself.

Same software, five hosts

The MDCC is the tactical-vehicle host of a family. The same Cortex software runs in an operations room, at a fixed command post, on a rugged edge server, or at higher headquarters. A formation therefore trains once and deploys the same behaviour at every echelon.
