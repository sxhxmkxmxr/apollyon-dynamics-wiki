Piranha USV, strike and surveillance from one hull

A low-observable, attritable unmanned surface vessel built on a common wave-piercing hull for strike and persistent maritime ISR. In its strike role, Piranha delivers a 280 kg shaped-charge warhead across 800+ km ranges using non-satellite maritime odometry and terminal optical tracking. In its dual-use role, it provides coastal agencies with persistent autonomous patrol without crewed vessels.

The platform is currently in structural integration, with sea trials scheduled before year-end. Navigation and control algorithms follow the core Apollyon doctrine: sovereign physical references, complete GNSS independence, and deterministic autonomy executed at the edge.

04 · Engineering specifics

Deep technical architecture

A · Non-satellite sea-surface navigation

Satellite denial over open water presents unique challenges: unlike land terrain, the open sea has no static topographic features for elevation correlation. The Piranha USV employs a layered navigation stack built specifically for marine environments:

  • CRPA Anti-Jam GNSS: Indigenous multi-element antenna suppresses coastal jammers by >45 dB, maintaining NavIC/GPS lock during initial transit.
  • Hydro-Inertial Dead Reckoning: High-grade tactical IMUs fused with Doppler Velocity Logs (DVL) measuring acoustic speed over seabed, filtering out surface current drift.
  • Coastline DSMAC: As the vessel nears target archipelagos, ports, or coastlines, high-resolution optical cameras match shoreline profiles, headlands, and navigation marks against pre-loaded commercial satellite maps.
  • Optical Waterline Tracking: In the terminal 3 km run, dual daylight/MWIR stabilised cameras lock onto ship hull silhouettes, ignoring decoys, chaff, and smoke screens.

B · Sea-state 4 hydrodynamic stability & waterjet control

Transiting at 45+ knots in rough seas induces extreme wave-slamming forces, propeller cavitation, and hull broaching. Apollyon's robust control stack solves these dynamics in real time:

  • Wave-Piercing Faceted Monohull: Sharp knife-bow pierces oncoming waves rather than riding over them, significantly reducing vertical G-loads on internal components.
  • Dynamic Waterjet Vectoring: Twin steerable waterjets eliminate exposed rudders and propellers, preventing damage from debris, nets, or shallow shoals.
  • Active Trim Control: High-speed hydraulic interceptor tabs respond at 100 Hz via the mission computer, continuously trimming pitch and roll to prevent porpoising at sprint speeds.

C · Heavy shaped-charge penetrating warhead

Unlike blast-only drone boats that produce superficial topside damage, the Piranha USV integrates a custom-engineered 250–300 kg shaped-charge warhead designed to compromise naval structural integrity:

  • Waterline & Sub-Surface Impact: The vessel trims down immediately prior to impact, detonating against the target's hull right at or below the waterline.
  • Copper Jet Penetration: The precision-machined shaped-charge liner drives a hyper-velocity jet through the hull below the waterline, flooding machinery and magazine spaces.
  • Redundant Fuzing: Redundant contact and delayed-action fuzing ensure lethal detonation even during glancing blows.
05 · Dual use

One hull, two customers

A common composite hull supports two distinct mission configurations. The strike variant houses a 280 kg shaped-charge warhead and impact fuzing. The surveillance variant replaces high explosives with an electro-optical/infrared mast, maritime search radar, and secure tactical datalinks. Port trusts, coast guards, and littoral security forces gain persistent presence across shipping lanes at a fraction of crewed vessel operating costs. Peacetime commercial manufacturing sustains shipyard tooling and workforce proficiency, establishing a rapid surge line for combat effectors when required.

Programme

First sea trials in 2026; full-scale trials with radar and imaging sensors in 2027; serial production from 2028. Hull tooling and the navigation package are common between the strike and surveillance variants, so the peacetime customer pays for the tooling the strike line inherits.
