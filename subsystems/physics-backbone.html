Physics Backbone & Sim-to-Real Engine

This technology has been consolidated into the unified primary subsystem authority: Robust Control for Fast Air Platforms.

Subsystem Consolidation Notice

The Physics Backbone and Sim-to-Real Loop are an indivisible part of our flight-control capability. To prevent architectural fragmentation, all documentation regarding instrumented telemetry calibration, mismatch identification, and direct-actuator neural training now resides centrally on the Robust Control for Fast Air Platforms specification page.

Key highlights of the unified stack:

  • Relentless calibration: Telemetry mismatches are traced to exact physical terms (aerodynamics, actuator thermal sag, structural resonance) rather than masked as process noise.
  • One backbone, modular airframe packages: Shared core across Ahuti, Nightshade ADX-1, HACM-350, and the Kamikaze USV.
  • Direct-actuator evaluation: 500 Hz neural policy trained against flight-grounded simulation.
